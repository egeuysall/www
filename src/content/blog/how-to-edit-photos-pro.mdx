### Learn the Settings First

Before you start editing, it’s important to get familiar with Lightroom’s key tools:

- **Exposure & Contrast**. Brighten or darken your image and make your subject stand out.
- **White Balance & Temperature**. Adjust colors to look natural or give your photo a specific mood.
- **Saturation & Vibrance**. Make colors pop without looking fake.
- **Crop & Straighten**. Improve your composition and remove distractions from the edges.
- **Sharpening & Noise Reduction**. Add clarity and smooth out any graininess.

Taking the time to learn these settings will make your edits faster and your photos more consistent.

### Editing Workflow (Step-by-Step)

Here’s a simple workflow I follow to edit photos like a pro:

1. **Start with Exposure & Contrast**. Adjust brightness and shadows for a balanced look.
2. **Fix White Balance**. Make colors accurate or create a mood with warmth or coolness.
3. **Enhance Colors**. Tweak saturation and vibrance to make the photo lively.
4. **Crop or Straighten**. Perfect your composition and make your subject stand out.
5. **Sharpen & Reduce Noise**. Add clarity and remove any grainy spots.
6. **Optional Preset**. Apply a preset to save time and get a consistent style.
