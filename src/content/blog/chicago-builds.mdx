# Chicago Builds

## Overview

**Chicago Builds** is a hackathon where students use technology to solve real problems affecting communities across Chicago.

## Prompt

> Identify a real problem in Chicago and build a practical technology-based solution.

Projects can focus on areas like:

- Transportation
- Education
- Accessibility
- Sustainability
- Public safety
- Community resources
- Small businesses

## Judging

- Community impact
- Technical execution
- Creativity
- Feasibility

## Tagline

**Build for Chicago.**
