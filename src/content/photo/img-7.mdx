This frame is mostly atmosphere. Detail drops fast in heavy snow, so the goal was to keep enough anchors in the foreground and let the rest dissolve.
The result is quiet and slightly flat, which is exactly how that morning felt. Some weather photos should keep the softness instead of fighting for clarity.
