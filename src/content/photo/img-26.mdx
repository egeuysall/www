We crossed to the Canadian side near sunset, and the whole scene felt louder than any photo can capture. The air was full of spray, and every gust changed how the falls looked through the lens.

I held this frame for the rhythm of the water rather than a single perfect moment. The mist kept shifting, people moved in and out of the edge of the composition, and then everything aligned for one quiet second before the roar took over again.
