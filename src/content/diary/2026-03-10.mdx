## Strategic clarity

The core issue is not awareness. It is a value-to-risk gap.

People are interested, but connecting a private GitHub repo feels like a high-trust action without enough proof. The demo solves this by showing concrete missing-decision output first.

A strong next move is simple: run Ryva on public repos, publish short screen recordings, and lead with outcomes instead of claims.

## External feedback

A founder on X sent me a 7-page breakdown of why my account/posts were underperforming. Most suggestions are fundamental, but likely effective. I plan to implement them directly and measure results.

If X underperforms after iteration, I will keep testing channels and re-balance back toward Reddit.
