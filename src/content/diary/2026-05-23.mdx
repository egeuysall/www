First full day in San Francisco. The Google I/O hackathon kicked off and I spent the entire day building [Talon](https://github.com/egeuysall/talon), an autonomous agent system with contextual memory. The environment was intimidating at first — everyone around me had PhDs, millions raised, years of experience. But as the day went on I realized most of these people weren't operating on some unreachable level. The difference was consistency and time spent building, not raw genius.

No time for ibx tracking. The entire focus was on building and absorbing the atmosphere. Waymos everywhere. Anthropic a minute away. Vercel billboards. Startup logos that only existed on my feed suddenly became physical objects surrounding me.
